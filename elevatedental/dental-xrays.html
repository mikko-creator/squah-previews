Digital Dental X-rays

Imaging is how a diagnosis gets made before a treatment is planned.

What this field covers

Four images, four questions

Four kinds of image are taken here. They are not four versions of one picture: they cover different amounts of you, they answer different questions, and only one of them is three-dimensional.

One or two teeth, from the crown to the tip of the root. Used to look for infection at the end of a root and to check the result of a root canal.

What a matrix cannot decide

Which image you need is decided by the question in front of your dentist, not by which one shows the most. A cone-beam volume is not a better periapical; it is a different instrument with a different dose, and it is used where depth actually changes the plan.

Nothing on this page is a request to have more imaging. The rule this practice works to is the one stated in the technology band: take the image that answers the question and no more than that.

04
What imaging finds

The questions a picture answers

Radiography is not a formality at the start of a plan. These are the findings it exists to catch, and most of them are invisible to an examination.

  • Hidden decay. Between teeth and underneath existing fillings.
  • Infection. Or a cyst at a root tip.
  • Bone loss. From gum disease, measured rather than estimated.
  • Impacted teeth. Unerupted ones included, wisdom teeth among them.
  • Bone volume. And nerve position, before an extraction or an implant.
  • Root angle. Position and angulation, before orthodontic treatment.
  • Old work. The state of a restoration or a previous root canal.
Why it matters that it is here

The reading does not wait on a third party.

Imaging is one of the nine fields listed on our treatments index, and it is done in-house. Your images are taken, read and used for planning inside the same appointment. Nothing is sent to an outside imaging center.

Digital Dental X-ray

Digital radiography and cone-beam scanning for diagnosis and treatment planning. Digital sensors replace film, so the image is on screen in seconds and the radiation dose is lower than conventional film radiography. Images are stored with your record, which means the next set can be compared against this one rather than read in isolation.

The specialists in this field

An image is only as useful as the clinician reading it

A panoramic image shows a shadow over a root. Whether that shadow is an artifact of the machine, a cyst, or a socket that is healing normally is a judgment call, and the judgment matters more than the picture.

Reads the periapical

Endodontics

The endodontist reads the close image before opening the tooth, and again during treatment to confirm working length and check the final seal.

Endodontics
Reads the volume

Oral Surgery

The oral surgeon reads the cone-beam volume before an extraction near the nerve, because the distance that decides the approach is measured in millimeters.

Oral Surgery
Reads the profile

Orthodontics

The orthodontist reads the side view before deciding how the jaws should relate, which is a measurement problem before it is a treatment plan.

Orthodontics

Why that matters

The clinician interpreting your scan is the clinician planning your treatment, which is not the case when imaging is bought as a standalone service from an outside center. Every specialist profile carries a full name, the field that clinician practices and their postnominal credentials.

07
Paying for it

One condition that is not a matter of preference

Elevate Dental does not accept HMO or dental insurance. All treatment is self-pay.

Imaging is usually taken at the same visit as the examination, which is exactly the point at which a payment condition should already be known rather than discovered.

01

Digital radiography

Direct digital capture in place of film. The picture appears in seconds, it can be enlarged and adjusted on screen while you are still in the chair, and the dose is lower than conventional film radiography.

02

iTero intraoral scanner

Records the surface shape of your teeth and gums as a digital impression. It replaces impression material, not radiography, and it uses no radiation at all.

03

RayFace facial scanner

Records the proportions of your face in three dimensions, for restorative and smile-design planning. It is a camera system rather than a radiographic one.

What this page does not print
