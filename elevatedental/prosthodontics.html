Prosthodontics Philippines: Crowns, Bridges, Dentures

Prosthodontics

Prosthodontics restores teeth that are damaged and replaces teeth that are gone.

Book an Appointment Compare all four appliances
02
What this field covers

Four situations, and what each one is answered with

A prosthodontist rebuilds what is missing or broken, and builds it so your bite can carry it for years.

  • A tooth too damaged to hold a filling. It is covered with a crown.
  • A gap where a tooth used to be. It is closed with a fixed bridge or, where the case allows, an implant restoration.
  • Several missing teeth in the same arch. They are replaced with a removable partial denture.
  • A jaw with no teeth left. It is restored with a complete denture.
The misreading this field corrects

The field also covers the bite itself.

Worn edges, a collapsed bite height and existing dentures that rock or rub are prosthodontic problems rather than cosmetic ones, and they are assessed as such. Prosthodontics is one of the nine fields of dentistry practiced in-house at Elevate Dental. That is why a case needing an extraction first, or a root canal first, does not move to a different clinic between steps. The plan is written once and carried out by the people who wrote it.

What a matrix cannot decide

Every row above is true of the appliance in general. None of it is true of your jaw until someone has read the bone, the bite and what is left of the teeth. Two people can arrive missing the same tooth and leave with different plans, because what holds the replacement is what actually chooses.

05
The honest half

When the groundwork comes first

A restoration is only as good as what holds it. Two conditions decide whether the appliance is made now or after something underneath it has been dealt with.

Build it now Ready

Make the restoration now when

what is left of the tooth is sound, the teeth that will carry the work are stable, and nothing under the gum is waiting to be treated. Then the remaining questions are material and fit, and those are settled against a scan and a bite reading rather than against an estimate.

Finish the groundwork Not yet

Do something else first when

the tooth needs a root canal before a crown can be built over it, or a tooth has to come out before an arch can be planned. An appliance made over unfinished work is measured against a mouth that is about to change shape.

Why a hub page says this out loud

Being told the sequence before the quote is not a delay and it is not an upsell. It is the difference between an appliance that seats and one that is remade in its first year, and you are better served hearing it here than at the chair with a plan already drawn.

Here the endodontist and the oral surgeon are colleagues on the same case, so a finding like that revises the sequence in one conversation rather than restarting it somewhere else.

06
The specialists in this field

Who else is in the room

Prosthodontics here is done by prosthodontists. That is the whole argument. A single-dentist practice sends this work out to somebody else; our patients stay with the team that assessed them.

These are the three fields a restorative case most often turns out to need before or beside the appliance itself.

If the nerve is involved

Endodontics

A tooth that needs the pulp removed before a crown can be built over it is an endodontic case first. The crown is designed after that work is sealed, not around it.

Endodontics
If a tooth has to go

Oral Surgery

An extraction, or an implant to carry a restoration, is surgical work planned on the imaging. Where a case allows an implant to carry the work, this is the field that places it.

Oral Surgery
If the foundation is unstable

Periodontics

The gum and bone a fixed restoration is anchored to. Where the supporting tissue is being treated, that treatment is settled before an appliance is measured to it.

07
Technology used here

What the appliance is measured against

Prosthodontic planning runs on named systems, not on unspecified equipment.

01

3D CBCT imaging

A three-dimensional scan of the jaw showing bone volume, root position and the nerve canal before a crown, bridge or implant restoration is planned.

02

iTero intraoral scanner

A digital impression taken with a handheld scanner instead of a tray of impression material. The scan travels to the laboratory as a file.

03

OccluSense bite analysis

A digital record of where your teeth meet and how hard. The restoration is adjusted against that reading rather than against an estimate.

04

RayFace facial scanner

A 3D scan of your face, used to plan crowns and smile design against your actual facial proportions rather than a generic template.

Imaging

The cone-beam scan is available at all four clinics: Mitsukoshi BGC, Shangri-La Plaza, Bonifacio Stopover BGC and Alabang Town Center. On this field it is what a bridge or an implant restoration is planned on, so the volume and the plan happen in one building.
