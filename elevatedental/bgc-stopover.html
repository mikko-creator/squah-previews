Finding Unit R303

Unit R303, 3rd floor, Bonifacio Stopover Pavilion, Bonifacio Global City, Taguig City.

Take the lift or stairs to the third floor and look for Unit R303. The unit number is the thing to remember at this address: the clinic is identified by its unit, not by a mall directory listing, so arriving on the right floor is only half of it.

  1. Reach the pavilion. Bonifacio Stopover Pavilion is the building to arrive at. It sits inside Bonifacio Global City, and the pavilion itself is the landmark rather than the district around it.
  2. Go to the third floor. Take the lift or the stairs. There is no separate street entrance to the clinic.
  3. Look for the unit, not the name. Unit R303 is the address to look for once you are on the floor, because a directory listing is not what identifies this clinic.
  4. If you drive. Parking is paid and in-building, so you can park and come up without leaving the building.

The bench
One group, four addresses

The specialists here

Elevate Dental is a group of specialists. Our specialists work by discipline, which means an orthodontic case is planned by an orthodontist, a root canal is performed by an endodontist, and a crown or bridge is designed by a prosthodontist.

A case opened here can be continued by the same group at another of our clinics without starting the assessment again.

Every clinic in the group runs the same protocols and the same records system, and draws on the same specialist bench. That is what makes a six-day clinic workable for a patient whose week does not always fit inside it.

The full roster, with each specialist's discipline, is published on the specialists page.

The technology here

3D CBCT imaging is installed at this clinic. CBCT is available at all four of our clinics, including this one, so the scan your case needs is taken where you are seen.

A CBCT scan shows bone volume, nerve position and root anatomy in three dimensions, which is what implant placement, impacted extractions and difficult root canals are planned against. Because it stands in the building, a case that needs one does not wait on an appointment somewhere else.
