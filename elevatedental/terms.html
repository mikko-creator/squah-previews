What the prices on this site are

The figures published across this site are representative market prices for each treatment. They are not this practice's own quoted fees, and no figure here is an offer or a quotation.

A real price depends on your case: what the assessment finds, which materials the plan uses, how many visits it takes. You get that figure from the clinic after an assessment, in writing, before treatment begins. If a number on this site and a number from the clinic disagree, the clinic's is the real one.

This is stated plainly because a published price is the easiest thing on a website to mistake for a promise.

What booking through this site does

The appointment form sends a request. It does not confirm an appointment, and submitting it does not hold a slot.

The clinics confirm by phone, which is why the form asks for a contact number. Until someone from the practice has spoken to you, treat a submitted form as a message received rather than a booking made.

What is not settled yet

A finished set of terms would cover the rest: how this site's content and images may be used, what the practice does and does not warrant about the information published here, how disputes are handled, and which law applies.

None of that is written here yet, because each of those is a decision for the practice and its adviser rather than something a website should assert on their behalf.
