One promise, every market

What changes by market

Search is decided market by market.

The people looking for what you sell in Denver are typing different words, in different volumes, against a different set of businesses than the people looking for it in Miami. That is real, and it is the part of local SEO that genuinely changes from one city to the next.

So the keyword set changes by market. We agree that set with you in writing, and it is built from what your city actually searches, not from a template with the city name dropped into it.

What does not change by market is the commitment or the number attached to it. We commit to a first-page outcome on the agreed keyword set, and that commitment reads the same in every one of these 25 metros. A harder market does not become a more expensive market here, and an easier one does not get a discount. There is no regional rate card, and there is no market we have to go away and price before we can talk to you.

We are not going to tell you how quickly a ranking arrives, in your city or in any other. That is the kind of promise that sounds strongest from the agency least able to keep it.

Owner gap

the guarantee terms, meaning which keywords qualify, the measurement window, the remedy if we miss and any exclusions, are to be published on the guarantee page and in the terms of service. Nothing about those mechanics is stated on this site until you supply them, in any market.
