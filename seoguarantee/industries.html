Why these pages exist

Specialisation here means the keyword set, not a different service.

Vertical pages usually imply that the work behind them changes. Ours does not, and saying so up front is more useful to you than pretending otherwise. A dental practice and a law firm get the same modules, in the same order, at the same published monthly price.

What genuinely changes is difficulty. A single-location practice competing inside a few square miles and a firm competing across an entire metro are not playing the same game, even though the method is the same. The number of competitors, the intent behind the search, the value of one converted enquiry and the strength of what already ranks all move by vertical. That is the honest reason these pages exist.

Which is why the qualifying keyword set is agreed with each client before work starts, rather than printed on a template page in advance. A guarantee attached to keywords nobody chose is a slogan. A guarantee attached to keywords you signed off on is a commitment, and that is the only version we are willing to sell.

Owner gap

the qualifying keyword definition is to be published here and on The Guarantee page. Which keywords count toward the guarantee, how they are agreed, over what window, with what remedy and with what exclusions. This build states none of those, because none have been supplied, and inventing them would make the one promise we charge for impossible to check.

01

Dental.

The contest is a map pack against practices a few streets away, and much of the demand is procedural rather than general. People search for implants, aligners or an emergency appointment, not for a practice by name. Ranking for your own name is not the job. Being there at the moment somebody decides they cannot wait is.
