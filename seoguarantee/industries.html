Why the trade decides it

A guarantee written against the wrong phrases would be worthless to us.

That is the reason this site carries five separate trade pages instead of one. The published case against guaranteed search work leans hardest on a single charge, that one-size-fits-all strategies do not work, and the charge is fair. It is also the charge we can least afford, because we are only paid while the agreed metrics are being delivered. An agency on an unconditional retainer can survive a generic phrase list indefinitely. We cannot. The incentive is the argument.

Four things change completely from one trade to the next, and they are what the pages below are built on.

The phrase set.

Not the wording, the vocabulary. A homeowner types the fault, a patient types the symptom, somebody in trouble types the event, and none of them types the name of the category they are about to buy from.

The buying moment.

An emergency at eleven at night and a purchase researched across three weeks are not the same demand wearing different clothes. One is settled before the second result finishes loading. The other is settled in a comparison you are not present for.

The geography that decides it.

A radius around one chair, a drive time a truck can actually cover, a metro, or a practice licensed across two states. The boundary is part of what gets contracted, and it is drawn differently in each of these five trades.

What a lead is worth.

This is what decides how hard a phrase is worth fighting for, and therefore which phrases we propose in the first place. A phrase worth a service call and a phrase worth a matter that runs for a year do not deserve the same effort.

The five doors

The five trades.

One thing is the same across all five: we propose the key phrases from competitive research and they are named in the contract you sign. Nothing else on this list is shared.

Dental.

Demand splits hard down the middle and the phrase set splits with it. Emergency dentistry and a tooth that broke tonight sit on one side, oral surgery in a named city on the other, and dentist near me is contested by every practice in the metro at once.

Law firm.

Organised by matter type and bounded by the jurisdictions a firm actually takes work in. Personal injury and car accident searches happen inside the hour; business law is compared across weeks. One matter can outweigh a year of the fee, which is why these are the most brutally contested phrases on this site.

Medical spa.

What is actually proven

The proof on this page is the contract, not a chart.

The retainer is agreed before the work starts. Each month is assessed against the agreed metrics. A month that comes up off-target pauses the billing while the work carries on at full scope, and the billing resumes once results are back on track. It needs no case study standing behind it.
