Why this page exists

A ranking guarantee invites skepticism, and it should.

Most owners have been promised results by an agency before, with nothing attached to the promise, so a commitment to the first page reads as either a trick or a technicality until somebody sits down and shows where the line is.

That explanation lands better on camera than in a paragraph. You can hear whether the answer is straight, whether the question was dodged, and whether the person answering it has to live with what he just said. So the hardest questions get their own video, including the ones that do not flatter us: what the guarantee does not cover, when we would turn the work down, and what happens when an agreed keyword turns out to be the wrong target.

What is settled today is the shape of the promise. We guarantee a first-page ranking for agreed keywords, and the full terms are to be published. That wording is deliberate, and it is the limit of what we will claim on camera.

Owner gap

OWNER UNSUPPLIED. The guarantee terms do not yet exist in writing. Which keywords qualify, the measurement window, the remedy if the ranking is not achieved, the exclusions and the contract length are all unsupplied. None of them is stated here, and none of them is to be spoken in any video. The guarantee videos cannot be finalized until the terms are supplied and published on the guarantee page.
