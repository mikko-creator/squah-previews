The loop, closed

How this connects to the guarantee.

We propose the key phrases from competitive research, they are named in the contract you sign, each month is assessed against the agreed metrics, and any month we are off target we keep working and your billing stops. Separately from the contract, the current site publishes 6- and 12-month windows, and the agreement itself is assessed month by month.

Guaranteed SEO is a term the industry publishes warnings about, and those warnings are earned by promises with nothing behind them. A guarantee with named metrics, a monthly test and a billing consequence is a different object from a promise with none of those: first page on the agreed key phrases, or you don't pay. We do not claim to control Google, and we claim no relationship with it.
