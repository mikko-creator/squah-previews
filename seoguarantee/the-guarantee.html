Why it holds up

Most agencies won't promise you a thing.

Read the fine print at the big shops and the pattern is the same. WebFX, one of the largest SEO agencies in the country, publishes that no one can guarantee a #1 ranking on Google. Victorious puts it in writing too: no SEO agency can guarantee you will rank for a particular keyword.

They are right that no one controls Google's algorithm. But you still sign the monthly contract, you still pay every month, and you carry all of the risk yourself. We took the other side of that deal. The algorithm is still Google's. The risk of missing is ours.

We do not take every business. The guarantee only works when the keywords are winnable and the fit is right, so we would rather tell you the truth up front than sign you into a promise we cannot keep.
