The Blez - +345% Organic Traffic

That is the headline number, and we lead with it rather than bury it. The Blez, a sports-cards e-commerce store, earned that lift under a bounded, money-back SEO guarantee with the writing folded into the one fee. What the number means, and how it happened, is the story underneath.

The challenge

Invisible for the searches that actually sell.

The Blez sells sports cards in a crowded, high-intent market where buyers know exactly what they are looking for and type it straight into search. The problem was simple and expensive: the store was not showing up for those category terms. Traffic that never sees you cannot buy from you.

That was the honest starting point. Not a broken site and not a bad product, just a business off the first page for the phrases its customers use, watching that demand land on competitors instead. A number like the one above only means something once you have seen the floor it started from.

The approach

Guaranteed SEO, with the content built in.

No secret lever and no overnight trick. The work was three deliberate moves under a single guaranteed-SEO engagement, aimed at exactly the terms buyers use.

01 / Agree the terms

The exact keywords, in writing

Before any work started we agreed the specific keywords the guarantee would cover. That named list is the thing you can hold us to - bounded and measurable, not a vague promise to do more SEO.

02 / Build the content

Pages written to earn those terms

We built and wrote the pages deliberately, targeted at exactly those agreed terms. Content writing is one of the services we fold into the guaranteed-SEO fee, not a line item we bill on top. It was a core part of the work, not an add-on.

03 / Hold the guarantee

Page one, or a full refund

The ranking carries a bounded promise tied to the named terms: reach page one for them inside the agreed window, or the fee is returned. It is those specific terms only, never a top-of-results boast and never a claim across all of search.

One flat fee for the guaranteed-SEO program - the content build is folded into it, so what you pay for is the guarantee, and the writing that earns it comes with the work rather than as an extra invoice.
