ThereforeWhy the guarantee exists

The gap described above is the reason for the model.

The operating rule the founder set is one sentence:

"We only do business one way, and one way only... And that's by guaranteeing results"

In practice that means the guarantee is the product, not a reassurance bolted onto a retainer. The fee is attached to a first-page ranking outcome for agreed keywords and phrases, and the rest of the stack comes with it at no extra charge. It also means the commitment has to be written down in public, in plain language, before anyone signs anything.

Owner gapGuarantee terms

the guarantee terms are not yet published. Which keywords qualify, the measurement window, the remedy if the ranking is not achieved, and the exclusions all need to be supplied and published on the guarantee page. Nothing about the terms is invented here or anywhere else on this site.

In practiceThe three rules

Three rules, and they are the ones you can hold us to.

Publish the price.

Our plans are listed on the pricing page with monthly figures in US dollars. You do not sit through a discovery call to find out what it costs. A published price is a checkable price, and a quote-gated one is not.

Commit to the outcome.

The agreement is built around a first-page ranking, not around hours logged or deliverables shipped. The full suite is bundled rather than billed a la carte, so the incentive is to move the ranking, not to add line items.

Say what is not covered.

Anything we cannot yet substantiate is marked as unsettled on this site instead of being dressed up. That is why you will see owner notes on this page rather than confident numbers we cannot back.
