What it replaces

What the fee replaces is a separate development retainer, or the agency-plus-dev-shop arrangement where the search team files requests and then waits on them.

One agreed monthly fee covers SEO, web, CRO, branding and design. Quoted per business after a competitive review.

Upstream of everything

Why the build decides whether the ranking is even possible.

Traffic, then leads, then sales. The build comes before all three.

A page that cannot be crawled or indexed cannot rank,

no matter how good the phrase research was. The build sits ahead of everything the contract commits us to, which makes it the first thing worth checking and the last thing worth assuming.

Speed is not a polish item.

A slow page loses the visit the position bought, and it drags the standing of the pages around it. Treating it as something to look at after launch is how a technically fine site quietly underperforms for a year.

Which is why the build sits inside the same contract.

If the site is the reason a metric is missed, and the site sits behind a separate quote, then the arrangement is hostage to a purchase you keep deferring. We removed that by including it.

None of this says a build produces a ranking. It says a build decides whether one is possible. The promise stays what it is, and it is set out as a term further down this page.

Standards

The technical standards we hold ourselves to.

These are commitments you can check rather than adjectives.

STANDARD 01

Indexability.

Every page we optimise is crawlable and indexable, and the pages that should not be indexed are excluded deliberately rather than left accidentally public.

STANDARD 02

Core Web Vitals as a build constraint.

Measured on real pages during the build, not asserted afterwards.

STANDARD 03

Semantic, valid markup,

with a heading structure that matches what the page actually says rather than what the theme happened to output.

STANDARD 04

Structured data that says what the page says.

The machine-readable metadata says what the page says, the condition on the fee included, rather than a stronger version of the promise.

STANDARD 05

Redirect and URL hygiene on any rebuild,

so a redesign does not cost positions that already exist.

Cadence

Ongoing development, not a one-off project.

A build here is not a delivery date. It is a standing capacity.

  1. A request goes in through the portal.
  2. It is scoped.
  3. It ships.
  4. The next monthly review covers what changed and what it did.

Two properties matter more than those steps. The work is continuous, so there is no handover after which the site starts ageing. And it does not stop in a month billing is paused, because the pause applies to the invoice rather than to the work.

The communication standard: you are told what shipped and why, every month.

The agreement itself is assessed month by month rather than as one test at the end.

The boundary

Where development stops and design starts.

  1. This page IS the build layer: templates, CMS, integrations, automations, speed, indexability.
  2. Web design owns the site as a designed artefact: templates as designed objects, brand application, the rebuild itself.
  3. UI/UX design owns flows, navigation behaviour and forms.
  4. CRO services owns measurement and testing of what those pages produce.
  5. SEO services owns the positions the whole arrangement is contracted to reach, and the competitive research behind them.

The split holds because all of it sits inside one contract and one agreed fee, which makes the boundary editorial rather than commercial. The phrases behind the whole arrangement are proposed by us from competitive research and named in the contract you sign.

The mechanic

How this is assessed and billed.

  1. A monthly retainer is agreed up front.
  2. Each month is assessed against the ranking metrics named in the contract.
  3. In a month we are off target, development continues and your billing stops.
  4. Billing resumes when results are back on track.

We keep working, you stop paying, and the obligation is suspended rather than erased.
