Coverage

The US Metros We Cover, All From One Las Vegas Office

We work with clients across US metros, all from our Las Vegas office. Before anything starts we agree the key phrases, and we agree the geography those phrases are measured in, so that the word delivered means something you can check.

The terms follow from that: first page on the agreed key phrases, or you don't pay. Any month we are off target we keep working and your billing stops, and it resumes when results are back on track.

The fair question

How we work a market we are not physically in.

If the desk is in Las Vegas, how does anyone rank a business in Cleveland.

The phrase set

The phrase set is local, so the work is local even when the desk is not. We start with competitive research in your market: which businesses hold page one there today, for which searches, and which of those searches actually produce enquiries for a business like yours. We propose, you sign, and the phrases are named in the contract. There is no veto or substitution right written into the agreement.

The measurement

The measurement is local too. The contract names the geography a position is measured in, not only the phrase. A ranking is not one number, it moves with where the search happens.

The address

A local mailing address does not move a ranking. Renting one in your city would add a line to a footer and nothing to your position.

What a local campaign actually covers.
