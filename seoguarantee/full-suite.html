Three fair questions

If everything is included, what is the catch?

The catch is that the guarantee is the product. We charge for a first-page ranking commitment, and the other five modules exist because they are what makes that commitment deliverable. It is not generosity. It is that we cannot rank a site we are not allowed to fix.

Is the extra work real or token?

It is the same work you would brief a specialist to do, done by the team that also owns the rankings. The panel above lists what each module produces. If something on that list is not being delivered on your account, that is a failure to raise with us, not a feature you did not buy.

Can you actually guarantee rankings?

There is a serious argument in this industry that nobody can, because the ranking algorithm belongs to Google and not to the agency selling you the promise. That argument deserves a straight answer rather than a brush-off. Our position is that a guarantee is only worth anything when it is specific: named keywords, a stated window, a stated remedy, stated exclusions. A vague promise is the thing the argument is right about.
