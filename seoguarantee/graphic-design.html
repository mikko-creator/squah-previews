The mechanic

How a request works.

Representative example.

  1. You submit the request through the client portal.
  2. It is scoped and queued.
  3. The asset comes back.
  4. Revisions are handled in the same thread rather than restarted as a new request.

Two properties matter more than the four steps. Requests are ongoing rather than a fixed allotment consumed at kick-off, so the tenth month works the same way as the first. And that does not change in a month billing is paused, because the pause applies to the invoice rather than to the work.

The real question

Why design is included instead of invoiced.

The question a sceptical buyer is actually asking is why this would be free. It is a fair question and it deserves the real answer rather than a warm one.

01

It is not free.

It sits inside the fee stated at the top of this page, and that fee is owed only while its condition is being met. You are paying for it. You are paying for it in the months we deliver, and not in the months we do not.

02

We are paid on the outcome rather than the hours.

Once that is the model, an hour of design costs us exactly what an hour of anything else costs us, and metering it back to you serves nobody. It would add an invoice, a conversation and a delay to work that only exists to make the campaign land.

03

The ordinary arrangement is the opposite.

Every agency in this market bills a monthly retainer and prices creative as a separate line or a change-order, which is why a design request usually begins with a quote and a wait. Ours is the one where the retainer stops being owed if the agreed metrics are not delivered, and where the creative work is not a separate purchase at all.

That comparison is structural only.

The mechanic

How this is assessed and billed.

  1. A monthly retainer is agreed up front.
  2. Each month is assessed against the ranking metrics named in the contract.
  3. In a month we are off target, your design requests keep being worked and your billing stops.
  4. Billing resumes when results are back on track.

We keep working, you stop paying. The obligation is suspended for that month rather than erased.
