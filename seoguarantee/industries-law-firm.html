The argument, in three parts.

THE ARITHMETIC

Rival firms hold your practice-area phrases, and the arithmetic here is unlike any other trade.

A single matter can be worth more than a year of the retainer, so a phrase moving onto page one is not a marginal gain in traffic. It is a different case load. We will not put a figure on that, because no reliable case-value figure exists in anything we are able to cite, and you know your own numbers considerably better than we would.

THE ENGAGEMENT

The phrases are named in the engagement before you sign it.

We propose the set from competitive research against the firms currently sitting on page one, and your firm signs it. There is no veto and no substitution right. A supplier selling contractual clarity to lawyers cannot afford to imply a right that the engagement will contradict at execution, so it is stated here rather than discovered later.

THE ASSESSMENT

The monthly assessment is what decides whether the invoice stands.

Not a review at the end of a term. That is the whole distance between this and the conventional arrangement, under which the retainer is owed regardless of what landed, and the only concession usually on the table is a right to terminate for the following month. Termination does nothing at all about the month that already missed.

The pause mechanic

Stated as a term, because a term is what it is.

The retainer is agreed at signing. Each month is assessed against the agreed metrics. A month that lands off-target pauses the billing, the scope of work continues without reduction, and billing resumes once results are back on track. Every clause of that is ruled by this firm's owner and is publishable today. It does not rest on a case study and it does not need one.

Here is the distinction worth landing precisely, because a pause can be mistaken for something it is not. This is an obligation suspended by a clause that was in the engagement before anyone signed, and it operates on its own the moment a month falls short. It is not a concession extracted afterwards by a client who complained. The published attack on this category is that a remedy agency can keep your initial investment anyway. The answer is that the billing stops under a term you agreed in advance, not that something gets handed back after an argument.

Method, and the lines we will not cross

Four charges, answered in order.

Written on the assumption that you have already read the case against guaranteed search work, noticed that it quotes the search engines' own documentation, and arrived here to see whether the counter-argument survives contact.

  • Search engines warn against ranking guarantees. They do, the warning describes a real pattern, and we are not going to argue with it. Nothing on this site suggests that any search engine approves of, permits or endorses an arrangement of this kind.
  • You cannot guarantee what you do not control. Correct. No one can guarantee a particular position, and this engagement does not purport to. What it guarantees is a term of business governing when a fee is owed.
  • Guaranteed work may involve tactics that put a site at risk. Answered by scope schedule rather than by assurance, and the schedule is printed below rather than described.
  • One-size-fits-all does not work. Answered by the map above rather than by a paragraph here.

Five questions

Answered in the register of an engagement letter.

Is a guaranteed ranking a red flag?

As a general proposition, yes, and a supplier promising you a position by a date deserves suspicion. The distinction here is what the promise attaches to. We are not making a representation about how a search engine will behave. We are stating when a fee becomes payable.

Who names the phrases?

We propose them from a read of the current page one and they are named in the engagement you sign. You do not hold a veto over the list, and it is not open to substitution once signed. That is a real limit on your side and you should meet it here rather than at execution.

What does a paused month actually mean for the work?

The scope continues in full. Nobody comes off the account, the schedule does not change, and the billing stops until the positions are back on track. It happens automatically under the clause, so there is nothing for you to invoke and nothing to negotiate.

How long?

The current site publishes 6- and 12-month windows, which are not yet an agreed contract term. The engagement is checked against the named phrases every month throughout, never as a single test at the end.

What happens on the call?

A review of your market, the practice areas and jurisdictions in scope, and a proposed phrase list. It is not a branded product, it does not have a name, and nothing is signed on it.
