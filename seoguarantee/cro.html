The failure02 / 09

Here is the failure this page exists to fix.

The traffic line climbs, the ranking report looks healthy, and the phone rings about as often as it did last quarter. Nothing in that picture is broken exactly. It is just not worth anything yet.

The reason is usually plain. Search brought a stranger to a page written for someone who had already decided. The page ranks. It does not ask for anything, or it asks in a way a busy person will not finish: a form with eleven fields, a phone number in an image, a next step three scrolls below the fold.

So we optimize for one action and we name it.

A contact, meaning a request for a consultation, by call or by form, from someone who is a fit for your business.

That is the conversion we count. Not time on page, not sessions, not a scroll depth chart that nobody spends money on.

If a month of work moved traffic and did not move consultation requests, the work did not land, and we would rather be measured on the number that turns into revenue than on the one that is easy to grow.

Three reasons03 / 09

Three reasons this work belongs inside the plan instead of beside it.

Included, not upsold.

All six modules in the plan are bundled at no extra charge, and conversion rate optimization is one of the six. It is not a second-phase engagement, an upgrade tier, or a line item that appears on your invoice once the rankings arrive. Every plan carries it, the entry plan included.

Tested rather than guessed.

A change here ships as a test, with a stated before and after against the one conversion goal above. Some tests win, some do nothing, and the ones that do nothing get reverted and recorded. We are not going to restyle a page on taste, watch a good month go by, and hand you the two as though one caused the other.

Wired to the team that controls the site.

The people who find the problem are the people who ship the fix, in the same plan, on the same monthly cadence. No change request, no separate build quote, no waiting for a development window that belongs to another vendor. That single fact is what makes iteration possible at all: when shipping a test costs a conversation instead of a purchase order, you get to run the second one.

Scope04 / 09

Conversion rate optimization is one of six services in every plan.

The other five are search engine optimization, website design and development, content writing, reputation management, and graphic design and branding. That matters more here than on any other service page, because conversion work is the module that consumes the others: it needs the site rebuilt, the copy rewritten and the review profile credible, and all three of those are already in the plan.

Inside the module, four areas of work.

  1. 1

    Funnel and form analysis.

    Which pages the traffic lands on, where people stall, which step loses them, and what the forms are actually asking for. Most enquiry forms are built around what the business wants to file, not what the visitor is willing to type.

  2. 2

    Message and offer testing.

    Whether the page answers the question that was typed into Google, and whether the thing being offered is a next step a stranger will take. The offer on this site is a free consultation, which is the shape of thing we look for on yours.

  3. 3

    Layout and button iteration.

    Where the next step sits, how obvious it is, and what it says. On this site there are exactly two next steps: Call Us and Learn More. Yours may need a different pair, but the discipline is the same, which is fewer choices, stated in plain language, placed where the reader already is.

  4. 4

    Call tracking alignment.

    Calls counted against the pages and keywords that produced them, so a ranking can be traced to a conversation rather than assumed to have caused one. This is where most reporting quietly breaks: the traffic data and the phone data live in different places and nobody joins them.

Common questions08 / 09

Is conversion rate optimization really included?

Yes, in all three plans. All six modules are bundled at no extra charge, so there is no conversion tier to add and no project fee for the work. We charge for the guarantee on your rankings and this comes with it.

How do you measure a lift?

Against the conversion goal named above, which is consultation requests, over a stated window before and after the change. Each test is recorded with what changed, when it shipped, and what happened to that number. Where call volume is part of the goal, calls are tracked to the pages that produced them so the before and after covers the phone as well as the forms. If a test cannot be measured that way, we say so instead of claiming it worked.

Does the guarantee cover conversions?

No. It is worth being blunt about this, because the two get blurred in this industry. The guarantee is a first-page ranking commitment on an agreed keyword set. It is not a commitment to a conversion rate, a lead volume, a revenue figure or a return on spend. Conversion work is included in your plan and we hold ourselves to the measurement above, but the thing our fee is guaranteed against is placement, not sales.
