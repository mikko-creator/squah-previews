The standard

How a result gets measured, and how it gets permission.

Four things, plainly.

01

What is measured, and in what.

First page means page one of Google organic results for an agreed key phrase in an agreed geography. Every position published here is read in a named tool, and the tool is named on the card rather than described in general terms. It is never guessed: it is supplied per result, or the result does not publish.

02

Permission.

Every named client gave written permission to be named. Where permission is not on file the outcome is not published, and that holds even when the outcome is a good one. This costs us cards, and it is the correct trade.

03

Individual results vary.

Nothing on this page predicts what will happen for a different business, in a different market, against a different set of competitors. A result is a record of one engagement, not a forecast for yours.

04

We publish no overall figure for how often campaigns reach first page.

The published case against services like this one is that the seller is vague and unfalsifiable, and the honest answer to that is not a better-sounding number. It is evidence where every individual claim carries a date, a method, a tool and a permission.

Backwards, and forwards

The same standard applies to everything already published.

Where an older result cannot be evidenced to this level, it is not published in a softer form. It is simply not published.

We do not claim an absolute rate of success in any vertical, and we make no claim about the sales you will make. Neither is substantiated, and neither belongs on an evidence page.
