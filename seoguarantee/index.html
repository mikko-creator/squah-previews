Proof Empty on purpose

The proof strip is empty on purpose.

This is where most agency homepages put a row of news logos, a star rating and a certification badge. Ours carries none of them today, and the reason is the same for all three: not one of them currently links to anything a reader could open and check.

  1. 01The Google review rating ships here the moment it is hyperlinked to the live Google Business Profile. Unlinked, a rating is a picture of a rating, so it stays off.
  2. 02The Google Partner badge ships here as a link to the live Google Partners directory listing, and not as a static image.
  3. 03Press mentions ship here as citations, one line each, with outlet, article title, date and a working link. The news marks on the current site have no link and no traceable article behind them, so they do not appear on this site in any form.

Why the plan exists Three faults

Three things that are normally wrong, and what we do instead.
